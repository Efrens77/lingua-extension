Lingua Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Lingua is a Chrome extension that helps users understand and learn from webpage text by translating pages and phrases, defining words, providing optional pronunciation and read-aloud features, and allowing users to save selected translations and vocabulary for study.

Information Lingua Processes

When you use Lingua, it may process text that you enter, select, highlight, request to translate, request to define, request to hear, or save for study. For Full Page Translation and Read Aloud, Lingua may process readable visible text from the webpage you are viewing. Lingua also stores your settings, recent Phrase translations, saved Learn translations, saved vocabulary, and temporary per-tab translation or playback state in Chrome storage on your device.

How Lingua Uses Information

Lingua uses this information only to provide its translation, definition, pronunciation, read-aloud, settings, and study features. Lingua does not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or use it for unrelated profiling.

Third-Party Services

Where available, Lingua may use Chrome's on-device language detection and translation capabilities. When an on-device capability is unavailable, text submitted for translation may be sent to Google Translate's public endpoint or MyMemory to obtain a translation. Words submitted for definitions or pronunciation may be sent to Free Dictionary, Wiktionary, and Wikimedia services. These services process requests under their own terms and privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy and the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy.

Lingua sends only the text needed for the feature you request. It does not send your saved Learn records to Lingua-controlled servers. If you choose the optional Send feedback link, information you submit through that separate Microsoft Forms page is handled by Microsoft and the information you provide there.

Local Storage and Your Choices

Your Lingua preferences, recent Phrase translations, and saved Learn data are stored locally in Chrome's extension storage. You can remove saved Learn entries from the Learn tab, clear recent Phrase translations in Lingua, or remove the extension through Chrome to remove its locally stored extension data.

Data Security

Lingua uses HTTPS for network requests to its translation and dictionary providers. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, but Lingua is designed to limit processing to the information needed for its stated features.

Children's Privacy

Lingua is not directed to children under the age at which consent is required under applicable law. Do not use Lingua to submit sensitive personal information.

Changes to This Policy

If Lingua's data practices change materially, this policy will be updated before or when those changes take effect. The Last updated date above indicates the latest revision.

Contact

For questions about this policy or Lingua, open an issue in the Lingua GitHub repository. The publisher contact email listed in the Chrome Web Store is also available for store-related contact.
